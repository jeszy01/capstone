# Withholding tax audit, October 25, 2026

Follow-up to `audit-2026-10-21.md`. Covers the Phase 1 findings, the fixes made under the approved plan (P1, P3, P5,
P7), the BIR research for the remaining items (P2, P4, P6), and the questions for the accountant. Nothing here is a
claim of BIR compliance.

## 1. Fixes made

| Item | Defect | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | A 13th month run has no regular pay, so it switched the employee to the cumulative-average method for the rest of the year and counted as a payroll period. | A 13th month run is a *benefit payment*: only the part above the remaining ₱90,000 is taxed, at the rate of the bracket of the employee's latest taxable regular compensation (RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.79(B)(3), Steps 3–4). An employee already on the cumulative-average method is taxed the increase in cumulative tax over the same number of periods. The run adds pay and tax to the year to date but no payroll period and no method switch, including for 13th month runs approved before the fix. | `withholdingTax.ts` (`benefitPaymentTax`), `taxHistory.ts`, `payrollCalculator.ts`, `computeRun.ts`, `WithholdingTaxReport.tsx` |
| **P3** | A withholding tax version could be edited in place after payroll runs had used it. | A version used by a non-rejected run cannot change its tables, ₱90,000 exemption or paid-leave setting, or be removed; a new version may only start after the last run that used the old one. Enforced in Payroll Settings and by the server on every save. The shipped weekly-table correction is still allowed. | `backend-render/withholdingRuleLock.js` (+ `.d.ts`), `backend-render/server.js`, `src/features/settings/SettingsEditors.tsx` |
| **P5** | Ordinary cutoffs counted runs still pending approval; if Finance rejected one, later runs kept tax based on it. | Only approved runs count (Approved, Funded, Exported, Paid, Closed), each once. A payroll cannot be submitted while an earlier run of the same year is not yet approved or rejected (Draft, Computed, Pending Approval or Returned). Submission recomputes tax from the current runs and asks for a recompute if it differs from the computed amounts. | `taxHistory.ts` (`taxHistoryRuns`, `earlierPendingBlocked`), `computeRun.ts` (`submitBlocked`) |
| **P7** | The government loan check estimated net pay without year-to-date withholding or annualization. | One row calculation (`calculateRunRow`) is used for the preview, the submission, the government loan check and the Withholding Tax estimates. | `taxHistory.ts`, `computeRun.ts`, `WithholdingTaxReport.tsx` |

Approved runs are not recalculated: their saved results are unchanged, and the server still refuses any change to a

## 2. BIR research

Sources read in full text: **RR 11-2018** (BIR, `bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Digest RR 11-2018.pdf`, 38 pages) and
**RR 2-98** as originally issued (copy at `offshore-management.com.ph`, not a BIR site; sections later amended are
noted). RR 29-2025 was verified from the BIR scan on October 21; its effective date was re-checked against PwC Tax Alert
No. 2 (2026) and Grant Thornton.

| Rule | Source and effective date | What it means for PBMS | Test / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tables apply to compensation **paid** | RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.79(B)(1) and Step 2: Annex E "for compensation paid starting January 1, 2023" | The table version and the tax year follow the **payment date**. PBMS uses the cutoff end (version) and start (year). | Q7 |
| Withholding applies to compensation **actually or constructively paid** | RR 2-98 Sec. 2.83.6 | An approved but unpaid payroll has not been withheld yet. PBMS counts approved runs in tax history and BIR worksheets whether or not they are paid. | Q8 |
| Regular vs supplementary | RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.79(B)(3) Step 1: regular = basic salary, fixed allowances; supplementary = commission, overtime, taxable retirement, taxable bonus, other taxable benefits, "with or without regard to a payroll period" | Matches PBMS, except holiday pay and leave pay, which the text does not classify. | Q5, Q6 |
| Bracket from regular pay, rate on total | Sec. 2.79(B)(3) Steps 3–4 | Matches PBMS (`withholdingTaxCore.test.ts`). | Verified |
| Cumulative-average method | Sec. 2.79(B)(5)(a): regular below the compensation level **but supplementary compensation is paid**; supplementary ≥ regular; new hire with a previous employer. Once used, it continues for the year | PBMS also switches when regular pay is low **and no supplementary pay is paid**. Totals agree, but the method then sticks for the year. | Q3 — not changed |
| Annualization | Sec. 2.79(B)(5)(b): on termination before year end and for the year-end adjustment; compensation paid since January 1, including previous employers | Matches PBMS. | Verified |
| Refund / deficiency | Sec. 2.79(B)(5)(b) Step 4: excess refunded by **January 25** of the next year, or with the last pay on termination; the refund may be deducted from that month's remittance. A deficiency is withheld from the last pay; if larger than the last pay, **the employer is liable** for the rest | PBMS refunds in the December payroll or the last pay (allowed). PBMS blocks a payroll whose net pay would be negative, so a deficiency larger than the last pay cannot be submitted. | Q9 |
| ₱90,000 benefits | RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.78.1(B)(11): 13th month pay; Christmas bonus, productivity incentives, loyalty award, gift in cash or in kind, and other benefits of similar nature, **paid or accrued** during the year | Commissions and other taxable pay do not qualify. | MWE eligibility | Sec. 2.78.1(B)(13): private-sector employee **paid the statutory minimum wage fixed by the RTWPB for the place where assigned** | PBMS uses one minimum wage setting and compares the daily rate (salary ÷ 22). No region, no wage-order history. | Q1 |
| MWE exempt pay | Sec. 2.78.1(B)(13), 2.79(A): SMW, holiday pay, overtime, night shift differential, **hazard pay** (only for actual danger/strife-torn/disease-infested/isolated assignments, DOLE-certified, reported in the 1604-C alphalist). ## 3. Questions for the accountant

- **Q1 — MWE eligibility.** Should PBMS record the region, or the wage order, each employee is paid under, with dated minimum wages, instead of one setting? An employee paid the SMW of their assigned place is an MWE; the daily rate ÷ 22 days is PBMS's approximation.
- **Q2 — MWE pay.** Is paid leave of an MWE part of the exempt SMW? Do employee contributions on exempt wages reduce the MWE's taxable additional compensation (taxable allowances, benefits above ₱90,000)? Does PBMS need hazard pay (with the DOLE certification) for any employee?
- **Q3 — Cumulative trigger.** RR 11-2018 requires supplementary compensation to be paid when regular pay is below the compensation level. Should PBMS stop switching employees whose regular pay is low but who receive no supplementary pay?
- **Q4 — Bonus categories.** Which bonus and incentive types does the company pay, and which fall under Christmas bonus, productivity incentive, loyalty award, gift or "similar nature" for the ₱90,000? PBMS would then replace the "non-taxable" checkbox with a category.
- **Q5 — Holiday pay.** Regular or supplementary compensation? (Matters for the bracket and the cumulative trigger, not the year total.)
- **Q6 — Paid leave.** Regular or supplementary compensation (the existing setting)?
- **Q7 — Dates.** Should the table version, the tax year and the 1601-C month all follow the **actual payment date**? A December 16–31 cutoff paid on January 5 would then belong to January (next year's) compensation.
- **Q8 — Unpaid runs.** Should an approved but not yet paid run be left out of tax history and BIR worksheets until it is paid?
- **Q9 — Deficiency larger than the last pay.** Should PBMS allow such a last pay (net pay ₱0, the rest recorded as the employer's liability), instead of blocking it?
- **Q10 — Rounding.** Is centavo rounding per period acceptable, or should the cumulative method round only the final figure?
- **Q11 — 13th month treatment (P1).** Confirm that supplementary compensation paid apart from regular pay is taxed at the rate of the bracket of the latest regular compensation.
- **Q12 — Refund netting on the 1601-C.** Report the December refund as a reduction of December's withholding (current) or on a separate adjustment line?

## 4. Proposal (not implemented): adjustment runs for approved payrolls (P6)

An approved run is never changed. A correction is a new run that references it.

- **Run type** `adjustment`, with `adjustsRunId` (the approved original), a required reason, and per employee the
  difference in each earning and deduction (positive or negative) and its recomputed withholding.
- **Workflow:** prepared by HR, submitted to Finance, approved or rejected like any run, then paid and released with
  its own payslip ("Adjustment to <cutoff>"). The server refuses an adjustment whose original is not approved, and any
  change to the original.
- **Tax:** the adjustment's pay is added to the year to date in the period it is **paid**. It is not a payroll period. Withholding follows the same rule as a separate supplementary payment, or the cumulative method, or the annualization in December. A correction of a previous year is out of scope; it needs amended BIR returns.
- **Reports:** the 1601-C worksheet counts the adjustment in its payment month; 2316 and the alphalist include it in
  the employee's year; the payroll register shows original and adjustment separately.
- **To decide first:** Q7 and Q8 (which date counts), which corrections are in scope (pay errors, deduction errors,
  tax errors), and how an overpayment is recovered.
