# Withholding tax fixes, October 28, 2026

Follow-up to `audit-2026-10-25.md`. This note does not claim BIR compliance. Items that depend on an interpretation
are listed in section 5 for the accountant; PBMS was not changed on a guess.

## 1. Defects fixed

| # | Defect | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The year-end adjustment ran only in a cutoff ending December 31. With the default cutoffs (26 – 9 and 10 – 25) no cutoff ends then, so the year was never annualized and over- or under-withheld tax was never settled. | The year-end payroll is the **last regular payroll paid in the year**, because the next cutoff is paid in the next year (`isYearEndRun`). With the default cutoffs it is Dec 10 – 25, paid Dec 31. With 1 – 15 / 16 – 31 it is still Dec 16 – 31. | `payrollRuns.ts`, `taxHistory.ts`, `computeRun.ts` |
| A2 | The tax year was the year of the cutoff's **start**, so Dec 26 – Jan 9 (paid Jan 15) was counted in the old year, and January of the new year started with no history. | The tax year is the year of the **pay date** (`taxYearOf`; runs saved without a pay date use their cutoff's pay date). The BIR tables apply to compensation *paid* (RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.79(B)(1)). Used for tax history, the submission checks, 13th month pay, the 1601-C month and the 2316 year. | `payrollRuns.ts`, `taxHistory.ts`, `computeRun.ts`, `thirteenthMonth.ts`, `birReports.ts` |
| A3 | The rule "release 13th month pay before the year-end payroll" looked for a run ending December 31, so it no longer worked. | It uses the year-end payroll from A1. | `computeRun.ts` |
| A4 | The cumulative-average method started when regular pay was below the first bracket even with **no** supplementary pay (and in a cutoff with no pay at all), and then stayed for the year. | It starts only when supplementary pay is paid and regular pay is below the first bracket or not more than the supplementary pay, or for a new hire with a previous employer (RR 11-2018 Sec. 2.79(B)(5)(a)). Answers Q3 from the RR text. | `withholdingTax.ts` |
| A5 | 13th month pay counted runs by start year, so the basic pay of the Dec 26 – Jan 9 cutoff was in no year's 13th month. | Runs count in the year they are paid: each cutoff is counted once. See Q13. | `thirteenthMonth.ts` |

**Baseline in Payroll Settings.** Withholding Tax now shows a **Rules and methods** table: every rule PBMS applies, its
current value and its legal basis, with "accountant" where it is a company choice still to be confirmed. Working Time
& Overtime shows the minimum wage orders.

**Approved and paid payrolls are not recalculated.** Their saved results stay as they are. A year that already
passed with the old rules keeps its figures. If the accountant finds a 2026 difference before the December payroll,
the December year-end adjustment (now working) settles it.

## 5. Not fixed: needs a decision

- **Q1 — Minimum wage by region.**
  - Employee records have no work location.
  - Wage orders apply company-wide, so the company's region must be entered.
  - The rate for small establishments (₱718 under NCR-28) is entered as the company's rate if it applies.
- **Q2 / B3 — Minimum wage earners.**
  - Open: whether their paid leave is exempt, and whether contributions reduce their taxable allowances.
  - For a minimum wage earner whose taxable pay is less than their contributions, the 2316 lines do not add up. PBMS now flags it.
- **Q4 — Bonus categories for the ₱90,000.** Today a bonus is either taxable or "non-taxable".
- **Q5 / Q6 — Holiday pay and paid leave.** Regular or supplementary pay. Both are supplementary today; paid leave has a setting.
- **Q7 — Actual payment date.**
  - The tax year follows the *scheduled* pay date of the run.
  - A run paid late (e.g. a December payroll paid in January) still counts on its scheduled date.
- **Q8 — Approved but unpaid runs.** Still counted, now flagged in the BIR worksheets.
- **Q9 — Deficiency larger than the last pay.** Still blocked.
- **Q10 — Rounding.** Still to the centavo each cutoff.
- **Q11 — Tax on 13th month above ₱90,000.**
- **Q12 — Refund on the 1601-C.**
- **Q13 — 13th month base.**
  - PBMS now counts the basic pay of payrolls paid in the year. Dec 26 – 31 pay counts in the next year's 13th month.
  - PD 851 says "basic salary earned within the calendar year". Splitting a cutoff by days would need daily pay, which runs do not keep.
- **De minimis limits.**
  - Verified (RR 29-2025) and shown in Payroll Settings.
  - Still in code (`deMinimis.ts`) as dated versions, not editable. Making them editable needs an editor and a lock like the tax tables.
- **Adjustment runs (P6).**
  - Proposal in `audit-2026-10-25.md` §4. Not built.
  - Today an approved payroll cannot be corrected inside PBMS.
- **Deleting approved runs.**
  - Commit `8ca20ea` (jeszy01, "for now") allows deleting approved and paid runs.
  - A deleted run disappears from tax history, 13th month and the BIR worksheets.
  - It should be reverted when the testing need is over; to agree with jeszy01.

## 6. Final audit before commit (Oct 28, 2026)

Second reading of two sources:
- **RR 11-2018:** the BIR digest (`bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Digest RR 11-2018.pdf`), read with `pdftotext`. It took effect on January 1, 2018; its Annex E table applies to compensation paid from January 1, 2023.
- **DOLE Handbook on Workers' Statutory Monetary Benefits** (2019 edition, NWPC).

### Corrections made in this audit

- **Cumulative-average trigger (A4).**
  - The RR text: regular compensation below the compensation level "but supplementary compensation is paid **during the calendar year**", or supplementary equal to or more than regular.
  - A4 had looked only at the current cutoff. It now also counts supplementary pay already paid earlier in the year (and benefits above ₱90,000).
  - New test: ₱837.45, then ₱0.
- **Minimum wage carried over from before.**
  - Wage orders are not saved yet, and the company had set its own minimum wage in the old single setting (not ₱695 or ₱755, the NCR rates PBMS shipped).
  - In that case that rate is kept for every date, instead of being replaced by NCR's.
  - New test.
- **1601-C refunds.** When December refunds exceed December withholding, the worksheet now says the rest is deducted from the following months' remittances, as Step 4 allows.

## 7. Designs for the remaining risks (not built)

### Work location and wage orders

- **Wage orders.** Keep them in Payroll Settings, keyed by **region and sector**. RTWPB rates differ by sector (NCR-28: ₱755 non-agriculture; ₱718 agriculture and small establishments). Each order has effective date, rate, wage order number and NWPC link.
- **Employee record.** Add the place of assignment (region) and the sector. The employee record comes from the HR system, so the field belongs there; PBMS can keep an override until HR sends it.
- **Payroll.** Pick the order for the employee's region and sector on the cutoff's days. An employee with no region is not treated as an MWE and is listed as a warning on the payroll (no silent NCR default).
- **Payroll row.** Save the wage order used on each row, so an approved payroll keeps it.

### De minimis limits

- **Today.** The values are dated versions in code (`deMinimis.ts`): RR 11-2018 and RR 29-2025 from January 6, 2026. They are verified and shown in Payroll Settings.
- **Proposal.** Move them to the withholding rule versions, with the same lock as the tax tables (a version used by a payroll cannot change; a new version starts after the last payroll that used the old one).
- **Risk.** Editable limits can be set wrong. Keep the RR 29-2025 values as the shipped default, and require a source citation on a new version.

### Adjustment runs

Extends the P6 proposal in `audit-2026-10-25.md` §4.

- **What it is.** A run of type `adjustment` linked to the approved original (`adjustsRunId`), with a required reason.
- **Rows.** Only the employees being corrected. Each earning and deduction is a signed difference; the withholding is recomputed. The original is never edited.
- **Tax.** Counted in the tax year and month it is **paid**. It is not a payroll period. Taxed like separately paid supplementary pay, or within the cumulative method, or in the year-end annualization if it is paid before or with the year-end payroll. A correction of a closed year needs amended BIR returns and is outside PBMS.
- **Workflow.** Same as a regular run: HR prepares, Finance approves (not the preparer), then paid, then its own payslip "Adjustment to <cutoff>". The server refuses an adjustment to a run that is not approved.
- **Reports.** The register shows the original and its adjustments together. The 1601-C uses the adjustment's pay month. The 2316 adds it to the year.

### Deleting approved payrolls (commit 8ca20ea)

- **What it does today.** Since that commit, any run can be deleted. The server removes the run from `payroll:runs`, deletes its funding receipt, its ESS payslips and its notifications, and logs one audit line.
- **What is lost.** Its pay and tax disappear from the year to date (later tax is computed without it), from 13th month pay and from the 1601-C/2316 worksheets. Employees lose payslips already released to them.
- **Proposed policy.**
  1. Only Draft, Computed, Returned and Rejected runs can be deleted, as before `8ca20ea`.
  2. An approved run is **voided** instead: Admin only, a reason required, Finance's confirmation.
     - A void is allowed only if nothing was paid. Once paid, a correction is an adjustment run.
     - The run stays in `payroll:runs` with status Voided, `voidedAt`, `voidedBy` and the reason.
     - The tax history and the BIR worksheets leave it out and list it as voided.
     - Payslips stay, marked void.
  3. The audit entry keeps the run's full JSON (rows, totals, tax), so a deleted or voided run can be reconstructed.
  4. For testing, use a separate test database or a demo-reset script, not production deletes.
- **Status.** To agree with jeszy01 before reverting their change.
