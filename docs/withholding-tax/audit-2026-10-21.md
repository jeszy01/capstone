# Withholding Tax audit — 21 October 2026

Audit of the PBMS withholding tax calculation (`src/features/payroll/withholdingTax.ts`, `payrollCalculator.ts`,
Review Payroll history) before any fix. The real PBMS functions were run against values computed independently from
the BIR tables effective January 1, 2023 onward. Raw results: `audit-2026-10-21-results.json` and
`audit-2026-10-21-december.json`. The BIR online calculator was not run (its page could not be opened from the audit
environment); expected values come from the BIR tables and the computation steps of RR 2-98 as amended.

## Risks found by code review

- No annualization when an employee separates before year end (RR 2-98 Sec. 2.79(B)(1)(b)(i) and 2.79.6).
- De minimis ceilings had been removed: any allowance marked non-taxable was fully exempt.
- Monetized unused vacation leave (de minimis up to 12 days a year under RR 29-2025) was fully taxed.
- Paid leave days are classified as supplementary compensation (regulation text does not state the classification).
- No BIR Form 2316, 1601-C or alphalist output.

The fixes, tests and remaining items for accountant review are in `accountant-review.md` and the commit history.
